Employee Leave Policy

Employees are allowed 20 days of paid leave annually. 
Leave must be applied at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal.
Sick leave is limited to 10 days per year and requires a medical certificate.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per government regulations.
Paternity leave is 5 days and must be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

Work From Home Policy

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Work from home is not allowed during the probation period of 3 months.
Employees must be available on all communication channels during work from home.

Code of Conduct

Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.
Harassment of any kind will result in immediate termination.
Confidential company information must not be shared outside the organization.
Dress code is business casual from Monday to Thursday, and casual on Fridays.

Salary and Appraisal

Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month.
Annual appraisals are conducted in April every year.
Performance bonuses are given based on quarterly targets.
